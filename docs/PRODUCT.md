# DegreeFlow

## Vision

DegreeFlow is an AI-powered academic companion designed to help university students understand their academic journey through one intelligent dashboard.

Instead of simply displaying marks and grades, DegreeFlow provides meaningful insights, degree progress tracking, AI recommendations, and graduation planning.

---

## Problem

Most university portals only provide raw academic information.

Students still don't know:

- Am I on track?
- Which subject should I clear next?
- What courses are blocking graduation?
- How close am I to finishing my degree?
- Am I placement ready?

---

## Solution

DegreeFlow transforms academic records into actionable insights.

Students can:

• Track degree completion

• View completed, failed and remaining courses

• Receive AI-powered recommendations

• Monitor placement readiness

• Plan graduation efficiently
